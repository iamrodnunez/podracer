Privacy Policy for Podracer

Last updated: February 18, 2025

Overview

Podracer ("the App") is a podcast player application. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Podcast Subscriptions: RSS feed URLs of podcasts you subscribe to
  • Episode Data: Metadata about episodes (titles, descriptions, playback progress)
  • Downloaded Episodes: Audio files you choose to download for offline listening
  • Playback History: Your listening history and progress
  • App Settings: Your preferences (playback speed, skip intervals, visualizer settings)

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect any personal information
  • We do not track your listening habits
  • We do not use analytics services
  • We do not display advertisements
  • We do not share any data with third parties

Network Requests

The App makes the following network requests:

  • iTunes Search API: To search for podcasts (search queries are sent to Apple's servers)
  • Podcast RSS Feeds: To fetch episode information from podcast publishers
  • Audio Streaming: To stream or download podcast episodes from podcast publishers

These requests are made directly to the respective services and we do not proxy or log this traffic.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using:

  • SQLite database for podcast and episode metadata
  • Local file system for downloaded episodes

No data is stored on external servers controlled by us.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the App or on our GitHub repository.
